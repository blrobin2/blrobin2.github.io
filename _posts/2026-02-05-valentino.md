The biopic stuff in this is tedious, and Rudolf Nureyev isn't half as good at acting as he is at dancing. The rest of the actors seem to be dragged down by his performance. The cinematography, costumes, and set design are fantastic, in classic Ken Russell form. Could've used more men dancing together.
