It's wild seeing the Robert Towne-isms, but rough around the edges (bordering on meaningless at times).

The movie has a solid setup — it just doesn't know how to follow through on the themes it sets up, pitting "rule of law" against "anarchy" in a post-apocalyptic context.

Betsy Jones-Moreland gets the least development despite having the juiciest throughline — in pre-apocalypse time, she never got the chance to choose her own life. In post-apocalypse...
