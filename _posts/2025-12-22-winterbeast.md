Wow, this movie managed to gross me out with its low-budget effects. The claymation looks like they started over with a new model and camera every time it happens

This movie is so hard to follow! It starts like it's the second episode after an action-packed pilot.

The Wild Goose Lodge owner is so unhinged, I love him so much.

- Ah, remember that scene with the tree branch?
- Wait, who's keeping an eye out for what? On what mountain? Where? What time is it? What the hell is going on?
- Am I a different actor than I was in the last shot? It’s hard to know. That's the fun of <i>Winterbeast</i>
- Ah, that universal experience every kid has — finding a grave in the woods with a box of Playboys inside.
