This movie was probably my first Vincent Price movie, and perhaps my first classic horror movie (as in, came out before the '80s)!

It's such a classic setup, and the scares are still effective in places (the lady in the basement?? C'mon!). Vincent Price is pitch-perfect — this role was practically made for him. Love the use of the Ennis House for the exteriors.

While some of the riffs made me chuckle, I decided just to vibe and not try to write down my favorites.
