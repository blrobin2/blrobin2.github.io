I love the opening theme song! It's so silly and cheerful.

The movie rivals US slow-burn sci-fi in how much time it spends watching the minutiae of ships taking off and landing.

But look, I kind of like this blend of space and kaiju movie. It looks nice, and the costumes, miniatures, and effects are entertaining. It has a vibe that I enjoy, even if the Rifftrax folks hated it.
