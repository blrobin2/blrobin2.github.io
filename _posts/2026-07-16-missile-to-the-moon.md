Is this a remake of <i><a href="https://boxd.it/UJg">Cat Women of the Moon</a></i>? I keep running into Cathy Downs in mediocre sci-fi. This movie starts soooo boring, but once they land on the moon, we get rock dudes that look like Gumbys melting on hot pavement and, like <i>Cat Women</i>, women.
