A sweet and sincere romance about learning to love after immense loss. The film is full of character moments and quirks that draw us in. The conflicts are small and understandable, and the film leans into romance tropes with intention.

The movie's biggest flaw is its treatment of David Allan Greer, whom it relegates to a supportive Black friend and a target of multiple microaggressions. Also, the film has a throwaway anti-trans joke that the filmmakers could easily have cut, saving it from the jarring rug pull.

I'm rating this highly because it made me cry: sadly, for Elizabeth's death at the beginning, and happily for Sydney the gorilla.

The movie believes in fate, and God is on many characters' lips. You'd have to believe in fate to believe this story, but if you can suspend your disbelief, your heart might heal a little.
