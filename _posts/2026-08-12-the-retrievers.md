Love the use of kung fu fight vocal dubs, especially when the cast is predominantly white.

This movie is so wildly violent! Lots of blood and torture. A soldier is chopping off a dude's head with hedge clippers by minute 4. They say "fuck" like the line is the last time they'll ever get to say it.

I feel like this influenced <i>Fateful Findings</i>.
