It's kind of crazy how poorly pieced together this movie is. Like, the cameras are nice, and the cast is recognizable and solid, but the filmmaking is almost uncanny in its bad cuts and nonsense script.

The story has such tragic underpinnings, only to become weirdly sad filler, with the real purpose of the movie being a glorified Nintendo commercial. Also, the "humor" sexualizes a child repeatedly.

<blockquote><i>I love the Power Glove. It's so bad.</i></blockquote>
