In our times of grief, we can forget how to be human. Or worse, we wish to forget but cannot. Here, our unnamed protagonist sees an opportunity to feign amnesia amid an amnesia pandemic. But as much as he wishes to hide from his past, it creeps back into him, showing its face in shopping habits and songs on the radio.

I think the film had an opportunity to say something more profound in its exploration—the setup and textures were there—but the film remained rather one-dimensional in its interpretation. Also, some of the things the doctors were prescribing him make sense on a metaphorical level, but not at the level the film was operating within. Finally, it has some twee moments that don't necessarily gel with the rest of the film's tone.

I found the film engaging, and I appreciated the journey it took us on. The performances were great, and the pacing is just right. I look forward to seeing Nikou's next film, <i>Fingernails</i>. I also found the total experience a little routine.
