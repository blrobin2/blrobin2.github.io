<blockquote><i>Have you ever done it with your dad?</i></blockquote>

Miki, a sex worker, takes the cap off the camcorder that her father, Kiyoshi, is holding. She also has a camera and takes candid shots of Kiyoshi. Kiyoshi just wants to make a documentary about "the youths." Slowly, Kiyoshi relents, touching her between her legs. She feels his erection and gives him a rundown on her pricing structure. So, he strips down, and the two have unsimulated, blurry sex. But the moment Kyoshi penetrates Miki, he ejaculates. So, she charges him double. Kiyoshi panics when he realizes the camera has been on the whole time.

<blockquote><i>Have you ever been hit on the head?</i></blockquote>

Kiyoshi arrives at a train station. Outside, the most Miike-looking character stands and smokes. Kiyoshi sees a father, mother, and young daughter walk by. The man outside opens the window and smashes Kiyoshi on the head with a rock. This man is Visitor Q.

<blockquote><i>Have you ever hit your mother?</i></blockquote>

Keiko, Miki's mother and Kiyoshi's wife, does a puzzle, her hands covered in cuts and bruises. The shoji are torn open as though punched through. Her son, Takuya, stomps down the stairs and immediately throws a vase at a glass cabinet, breaking it. He whips his mother with a rug beater and overturns her puzzle, all for buying him the wrong toothbrush. He stomps back upstairs.  Keiko limps into her bedroom, carefully undressing so as not to reopen any cuts, and pulls out her supplies to shoot up heroin.

The film follows this family as Visitor Q stays with them for a while.

<blockquote><i>I realized something when you were holding me. I'm not a special woman or a pathetic woman. I'm just an ordinary woman.</i></blockquote>

The film is super low budget, part of a series of straight-to-video releases that explore the cost benefits of shooting digital video. Miike's approach is to hit all the transgression heavy-hitters: incest, domestic abuse, rape, necrophilia, body excretions, and more. Did I mention this film is a comedy? It does everything at such an absurd level that the laughs almost involuntarily pour out.

The aim appears to use Japanese porn categories to highlight how families put their misery onto one another. The film, despite everything, is trying to fix the nuclear family. If the family can put their misery elsewhere and stop trying to repress themselves, they may learn how to love one another again.

<blockquote><i>I couldn't understand how I really felt. But now, I finally understand. It wasn't anger, and it wasn't sadness. I felt it down here, and I wanted to have sex. Some things are truly strange.</i></blockquote>

The sound design is ridiculous. The squeeze bottle ejaculation, the ripping fabric "penis caught in a vagina during rigor mortis," and subsequent corking a bottle "pulling penis out."

While this movie isn't the most transgressive and disgusting movie I've watched, it's on the shortlist. Somewhere between the first lactation scene and the bullies forcing Takuya to defecate, I found myself appreciating this absurdist domestic horror. I wouldn't really recommend it, though, unless you're already going to watch it and no one can stop you.
