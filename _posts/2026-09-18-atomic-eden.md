The coldest open, immediately into a shootout with a bunch of strangers and a Wilhelm Scream.

So this is an <i>Expendables</i> rip-off, but the only person I recognize is Fred Williamson 😢

I feel like I should give this an extra half-star for all the dudes on fire throughout the movie. But also, it's way too many dudes on fire for one movie.
