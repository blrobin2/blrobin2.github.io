What a miracle of a movie! I don't think I fully appreciated all the layers in play when I first watched this — in fact, a moment I remember rolling my eyes at last time made me tear up this time around. The film deftly balances the harsh realities of life with moments of magic and hope. I'm still uncomfortable with the f-slur and the misgendering of the trans character, but that is also a harsh reality, isn't it?
