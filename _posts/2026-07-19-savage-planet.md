In the year 2068, four members of Project Arc navigate the surface of Planet Oxygen. Their objective? Secure resources for the dying planet Earth. While trekking through the lush forests, one member accidentally cuts off another's hand with a machete. He falls into a pit, and his hand stump falls into a pit of mysterious liquid that causes his hand to regrow. Then, a bear!!

Cut to Randall waking up on his first day of vacation. Carlson, the richest man on the planet, issues an order for Randal, John, Carter, and newbie biologist Allison to assist with Project Arc. They meet with Duncan, who gives the cast the rundown the interstitials gave us at the beginning of the movie. Planet Oxygen's wildlife and vegetation are 99.9% similar to Earth (convenient for the movie's production team). Corporations have invested in the discovery of a plant that can produce a planet's worth of oxygen.

How will they get there? DST, or deep-space travel. Their sensors suggest that the biggest animal they will encounter is a rabbit—certainly no bears or anything like that. The team is also bringing a wildcard (he has a face tattoo!!). But what happened to the last team??

The diet Tony Scott cinematography and editing are trying so hard to make this movie exciting. Cutting down vines — zoom! Wake up and turn off alarm — slo-mo recap! Duncan being sassy — extreme close up!

The movie has a solid theme around the fatal flaw of relying on corporations to save or protect Earth. If they can capitalize on a solution, they will put every roadblock in the way to ensure they get premier access.

Otherwise, the movie is fun in that good-bad sort of way, but not enough that I would suggest it.
