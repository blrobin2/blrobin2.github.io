The film establishes a grounding in martial arts, presented mostly straight, before engaging in filmic distortions. The dance leaps across space, leaving the artificial studio for the natural surroundings, bringing the movement into the world. Time slows, images freeze, and the dance reverses, as though the dancer is rescinding the violence. Deren's love of dance gives this one a more documentary feel than some of her others, but she never abandons experimenting with the materials.
