The problem with any bad movie, no matter its reputation, is that it draws in sickos like me, who have to know just how bad it actually is. Life is short, but during this movie's 72-minute runtime, it has never been longer.

The production design is where the film excels—makes sense, considering director Perry Andelin Blake has made a career in production design, and this is his only directorial effort.

The humor is childish, and if it stayed there, it might have been more tolerable, but with the brown face, misogyny, and overall mean-spiritedness, the amusement fades quickly.

I'm happy for Brent Spiner to get to be a ridiculous moron outside of the confines of <i>TNG</i>. But his only joke seemed to be farting.

The music cues are so bland — Walking on Sunshine, I Like to Move It, Eye of the Tiger, a remix of Walking on Sunshine, Do the Conga, Can't Touch This. Also, so many Master of Disguise songs were written for this movie.

Did they film this on the same exterior set they used for How I Met Your Mother?
