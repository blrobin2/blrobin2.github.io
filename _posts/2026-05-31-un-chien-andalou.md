Dream logic is a fascinating way to organize a film, in part, because the filmmaker has no real obligation to make sense. That said, it helps when the viewer can map a meaning, even if that meaning belongs entirely to them. In my past viewings of this short, I haven't tried to attach meaning to it, and I've felt disconnected from it. I will blame that in part on my disdain for Fascist scum Salvador Dalí.

The meaning I derive from the film is that it is about the wounds we take and the parts of ourselves we lose in childhood and through relationships. Through the window, we see a flashback to when he showed off the severed hand and drew a crowd, only to lock it up in his heart. A car hits and kills that childlike self, and the man begins to attack the woman. We also see a father removing the feminine fabrics so that he presents more male, and ultimately, his future self killing off that part of him. The young woman escapes that abusive relationship and enters one where they can freely navigate the world. When the past comes up, they help one another sift through the difficult stuff and move on.

I'm sure your experience will be different!
