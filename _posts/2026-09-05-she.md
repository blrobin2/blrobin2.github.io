The art and costume direction is pretty solid — the dresses are particularly excellent! The special effects for the sacred flame are sick. That said, the sabertooth tiger in the ice looks like it was designed by a medieval painter who's never seen one before.

This movie was nominated for an Academy Award for dance direction. The first time we see dancing is white people in nativeface shrugging their shoulders. Thankfully, that's not the award-nominated dance.

SHE wears a sick outfit that looks like the evil queen from the Snow White Disney cartoon. She's evil AF — she's also the only interesting character in the movie. Everyone else is cardboard.

The script is a MESS. From the same writer as the original <i>King Kong</i> movies, Ruth Rose. How many adaptations of this story have there been?
