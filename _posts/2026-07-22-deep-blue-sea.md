Yes, it's a silly action flick with tons of highly implausible scenarios. But it keeps the tension ratcheted up and never lets anyone make a move without consequences. The setting is perfect for a unique collection of set pieces, even if getting to those set pieces took a couple of leaps (sometimes literally).

What makes the movie most memorable to me is Preacher's belief that the sharks are God's punishment. Susan played god, and the whole team suffers for it. It comes up in small ways too. Jim tries to sneak a smoke and loses an arm — Preacher finds a Playboy, and the shark gets hot on his tail.

In my fan fiction, Bird saves the day, riding the last shark into electrified water before calling it a big butt.
