A moldy and surreal revisit with Marthe Jarnias, who played Old Aunt Lydie in <i><a href="https://boxd.it/eNsief">Vagabond</a></i>, made of archival footage and later narration from Varda. Old Aunt Lydie is probably my favorite minor character in the movie because she comes the closest to seeing Mona as a human being.

<blockquote><i>In the laughter which unites this rich lady and this poor girl, their social classes cease to exist. It is also a chance for Marthe, through laughter, to get a kind of revenge on her life of labor.</i></blockquote>
