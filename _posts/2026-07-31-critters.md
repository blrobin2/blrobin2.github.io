As a <a href="https://boxd.it/4arKc9" rel="nofollow"><i>Gremlins</i></a> rip-off, this movie excels in ways that <i>Gremlins</i> does not, while still maintaining its fair share of problems. Both have tremendous special effects that excel in their own ways. The Crites are fun designs, with their rolling furball transportation and poisonous quills. The gore is pretty graphic for PG-13, but I suppose the rating meant something different in the 80s.

I still have qualms. Most notably, the bounty hunters are nonsense — their transformation effects are sick, but why they so haphazardly shoot up random places is just confounding. Also, the plot, at its core, is fairly conventional for the genre.

Still, I came away having a good time.
