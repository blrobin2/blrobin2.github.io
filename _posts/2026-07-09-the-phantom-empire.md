<blockquote><i>The story you are about to see is TRUE. Nothing has been changed. These are the real people who lived this great Adventure and this is EXACTLY the way it really happened.... Just as they told it to me. I really mean this.</i> — Fred Olen Ray</blockquote>

A growl emits from a glowing red cave. In a nearby park, a couple packs up their day out, calling for their dog, Simon. The man takes a stick and looks for the dog, ready to beat it for not coming—a yelp. The man finds the dog ripped to shreds. A monster attacks the woman. The man throws his stick at the monster, and the monster rips his head off. The woman bludgeons the monster with her cooler, killing it.

Cort and Eddy work at C&C Salvage Company (not to be confused with C+C Music Factory), and work has been non-existent. That is, until a knock at the door from Denea Chambers, toting a newspaper article about the monster slain in the park. What interests Denea, however, are the jewels found on the monster's body. She believes the stones came from Rilya, a supposed kingdom found deep beneath the Earth's surface.

Still, Denea offers Cort and Eddy $3K. They get a map from the eccentric field geologist Bill, who spent time down in the caves and was the only one to survive his expedition. Joining them are Andrew Paris and Professor Strock. What will they find in those dangerous caves? Will they live to tell about it?

The movie makes it clear from moment one that it isn't taking any of this seriously. It's chasing the absurdist fun of the 30s serial from which the filmmakers took the title. It's cheap, it's silly, and it's mostly entertaining, but its turgid pace and hammy effects might scare away casual viewers.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- Some headlines from the fake Weekly World News-esque newspaper that Denea brings in: "Ghostly couple roam old church in Seattle!", "Boy, 15, delivers his girlfriend's baby as parents watch TV!", "Surgery patients wake up — in the morgue!"
- Love a Russ Tamblyn appearance! No surprise, he's a weirdo in this too.
- Yay, Jeffrey Combs! He's so adorable, and his pants are way too tight to do all the running about he has to. Of course his character works at the Miskatonic Institute — always close to HP Lovecraft. All the women are hot for him. Can't say I blame them.
- They actually reuse the Robby the Robot from <i>Forbidden Planet</i>, but outfitted with a new head! This movie is full of recycled props from other movies.
- Sybil Danning rides up in her weird space cruiser. I like that she beats the shit out of Cort.
- Hell yeah, dinosaurs. Of course, they're all spliced in from <i><a href="https://boxd.it/9bf6tz">Planet of Dinosaurs</a></i>.
