While it's hard to recapture the feeling of sitting in the theater and watching it for the first time, the movie (obviously) holds up. What's more, it has survived the onslaught of multiverse slop that Marvel has been churning out and remains a fun exploration of that concept.

Unless something changes in the corporate hellscape of Marvel Studios, this will remain the best superhero movie ever made.
