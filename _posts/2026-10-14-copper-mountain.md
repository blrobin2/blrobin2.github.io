Notorious Jim Carrey flick I never expected to see, but Rifftrax knows how to take me to all sorts of hell. The whole thing is a thinly-veiled Club Med infomercial. Jim Carrey does bits and impersonations from his stand-up routine. Ronnie Hawkins performs some CCR covers. Alan Thicke skis.

If this is on your watchlist, take it off. It's not worth it, I promise. I also understand that this phrasing makes it bait for folks like me.
