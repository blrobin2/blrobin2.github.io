The film is three documentaries competing for the runtime: a biography of George Romero, the story of Biohazard/Resident Evil, and the minutiae of Romero working on an adaptation. It’s all interesting in some respects, but the doc ends up feeling bloated and unfocused. It makes sense that this was probably funded and made through the George A. Romero foundation that the film features prominently — it's cheap, the filmmakers seem to have no experience, but the content is heartfelt and deeply for the fans. Watching the movie with a superfan helped me appreciate the earnestness of the project — their complaints about the length and lack of focus made me feel all the more confident that this just isn't very good.
