My second David DeCoteau after <i>Sorority Babes in the Slimeball Bowl-a-Rama</i>, this film is decidedly less horny.

I love that the movie believes every scene needs a cutaway to the beach, the woods, or a waterfall between them, lest we become overwhelmed by the non-stop action.

The talking cat sounds like Eric Roberts in a hotel bathroom — that's because it is absolutely Eric Roberts, though the bathroom used for the recording is left to our speculation.

The Rifftrax guys did not seem to understand the very basic rules around how/when Duffy talks. The rules are pretty arbitrary, but they're not incoherent.
