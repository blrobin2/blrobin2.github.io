Alamo continues its precipitous decline in quality as a rainstorm takes out the power, then the sound slowly degrades before getting a rain check.

Speaking of enshitification, the film (or at least what I saw of it) explores where money goes and how basic infrastructure keeps degrading, with NYC concrete as the cornerstone. Concrete is so lucrative, yet the money doesn't seem to flow back to reinforcing or rebuilding existing structures, leading to chronic flooding and power outages. The same goes for the entertainment industry.

I look forward to seeing the end of this movie, but it will have to wait for streaming or other non-theater options.
