This isn't so much a movie as it is a 2.5-hour music video—2000s CGI, bright colors, and trippy inversions for songs like "I Am the Walrus." There isn't really a story. We see characters named after Beatles songs in situations inspired by them.

The film uses the Vietnam War as a backdrop, but what is it saying beyond "War, am I right?" Jude is an apolitical artist who just wants to get high with a little help from his friends. Lucy is ready to be radical until asked.

I get the appeal, but I had a hard time connecting with anything on screen. I don't know what I would ask of a movie like this. I'm not a huge fan of jukebox musicals as it is, so maybe this one never stood a chance of winning me over.
