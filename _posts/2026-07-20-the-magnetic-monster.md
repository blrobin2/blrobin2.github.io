In this movie, folks are pretty cavalier about radiation.

I like the idea of a monster movie where the monster isn't a metaphor, but the actual hubris of science. The film seems to be about the US and the proliferation of nuclear armaments during the Cold War (like so much 50s sci-fi). But the film smartly distinguishes between science as a tool for improving human lives and the greedy pursuit of a false sense of security.

The microscope footage is pretty sick. Also, the climax is a visual feast, even though I have no idea what's going on — it's the equivalent of present-day films having swirling vortexes of CGI to suggest something big is happening.

The problem with the movie is that it’s basically a radio play outside of 2-3 set pieces. But I felt invested and on the film's side throughout.
