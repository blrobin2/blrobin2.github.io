If you, like me, believe that capitalism is actively working against global warming mitigation efforts, it will dishearten you further to know just how invested the US military is in accelerating it. For example, the doc shows primary-source footage of military figures citing the resource-mining opportunities the melting polar ice caps offer. We see millions spent on "green initiatives" in the military, such as increasing tank gas mileage from .75 mpg to .9. We see the effects of the military abandoning and dumping radioactive waste, often on the sites of their military outposts, dooming all its residents to genetic defects and cancers. And much more.

Or perhaps you knew this already. Regardless, this two-hour documentary packs a lot of meaningful information and documents into its runtime.

While the doc is one I agree with pretty much wholeheartedly, I worry about the sensational nature of some of its moments and the emotionally manipulative editing techniques. The doc rightly states that numbers are numbing, giving us enough to listen to the footage that it later documents, but I can see some viewers balking at the film's more emotional appeals. We're dealing with an urgent and horrifying situation, though, so I believe emotional appeals are necessary.

Send this one to your bleeding-heart liberal friends and family, and you may find them inching a bit more to the left.
