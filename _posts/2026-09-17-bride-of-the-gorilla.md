When Raymond Burr looks in the mirror and sees himself as a gorilla, that's cinema, baby.

Lon Chaney Jr. plays a "jungle native" who went to university. Thankfully, the film makes no effort to change his race or appearance.

Is it dated and silly? Yes. But as a film, its story has some compelling components that kept me interested in seeing how it turned out.
