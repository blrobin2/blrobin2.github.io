I had a friend like Lawrence in elementary school. His name was Jonathan Rakowski. He would make fun of my clothes or bring over video games for him to play alone at my place. I don't think he had any idea how to care about anyone. I was so socially awkward that I accepted anyone who would be my friend. I don't remember why we stopped hanging out — I don't think we had any sort of falling out. I think he went to a military academy.

I appreciate that Lawrence and Alana intersected in each other's lives weirdly and ended up changing it for the better by showing their worst selves to one another. That said, I don't know if the movie earns its ending — it felt like the movie was more interested in adhering to the coming-of-age formula than finding a genuine way for Lawrence to arrive where he does.

Isaiah Lehtinen and Romina D'Ugo give such compelling performances! I imagine many people will find them off-putting in one way or another, but I like seeing frustrating and complicated people in movies.
