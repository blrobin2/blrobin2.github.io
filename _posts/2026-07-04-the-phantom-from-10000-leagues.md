Maybe the most misleading title? It’s a mutated sea snake from a reef just off the coast of California. But I suppose the "legend" around town was more phantom-y.

The movie is pretty dry. Like most movies from this era with scuba footage, they seem hellbent on using all of it. Good news—everyone has nice thighs and shoulders.

Of course the monster is a little doofy, but he's far from the doofiest I've seen. Well, not that far.

Ethel is the best character — she's self-motivated and headstrong, but being a woman of that demeanor in the 50s is asking for trouble.

The movie made sure the teen boys in the audience got to see a bra and a bathing suit.
