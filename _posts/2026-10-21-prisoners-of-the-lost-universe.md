TV actors Richard Hatch and Kay Lenz do their best with the material. The movie has some scope and decent costumes, but despite the insistent, adventurous score, it's pretty boring. That said, I could see kids from the era having a decent time with this one.

Richard Hatch plays a plumber named Dan. Perhaps this movie inspired the <a href="https://boxd.it/g0UiAB" title="Super Mario Bros."><i>Super Mario Bros.</i></a> movie, where a plumber named Mario is transported to a parallel universe where nonsense happens.
