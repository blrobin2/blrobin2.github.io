This film was pretty delightful and easygoing, but it has so much squandered potential! We have Maxine and Patricia, two able and curious women, who could have solved all of this… but nay, a man (the same man!) had to come in and rescue them both. To further prevent the women from accomplishing anything, we have a man in a gorilla costume whose sole purpose seems to be to serve as a "woman foil." Still, maybe the best Sam Newfield flick I've seen.
