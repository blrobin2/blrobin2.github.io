NBC commissioned Fellini to produce an hour-long episode for their show NBC Experiment in Television. Fellini focuses on an abandoned project, The Voyage of G. Mastorna, as well as Fellini Satyricon, exploring the random places that inspire him. In true Fellini form, he creates a fiction around himself and his process, indulging and subverting the notion of documentary.

While I love a couple of Fellini films, I wouldn't call myself his biggest fan. I've enjoyed his 50s output the most, where he was still walking the line between Italian Neorealism and the personal surrealism that would later dominate his work. Still, I have a dozen or so of his movies that I would like to check out, so my opinion may change.

My biggest issue with this piece is that NBC expected the piece to be in English. Perhaps this was later remastered for the Criterion Fellini box set, but when I downloaded it in college, I got the TV-quality version. There is still plenty to appreciate, but I wouldn't recommend this to anyone other than Fellini diehards.
