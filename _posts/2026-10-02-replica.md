From the director of <i>Birdemic</i> comes a movie about THERAPEUTIC CLONING. And just like <i>Birdemic</i>, this movie features so many scenes of cars getting from one place to another. But it also features a lot of terrible/amazing digital backgrounds. The house where they filmed several scenes has a bird that won't shut up.

Put this alongside Vertigo as one of the great SF movies. Also put it alongside Vertigo because it inexplicably borrows plot points from—excuse me, it's an homage to Vertigo. The main character is a self-proclaimed Hitchcock fan.
