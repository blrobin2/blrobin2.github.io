I wanted to watch this again because I've been listening to "Butterfly" by Crazy Town on repeat for weeks, bordering on months. It's an unhealthy obsession with an objectively stupid song, but it hits.

As I started watching it, it also became a way to honor the late great Catherine O'Hara. Here, she gives us a perfect glimpse of what Kevin McCallister's mom would turn into when Kevin got that age. And Buzz would absolutely turn into Jack Black's character.

<a href="https://boxd.it/3pq4Lh">As I said in my previous review</a>, I love this movie beyond any logical reason. It was a movie that my friend Ben Bogardus and I just decided to love and support. We also did that with <i>The Lizzie McGuire Movie</i>, if that gives you any insight into our tastemaker aesthetics.

Perfect cozy snow day movie.
