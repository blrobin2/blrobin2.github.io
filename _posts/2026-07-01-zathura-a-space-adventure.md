Like on his podcast, Dax Shepherd interrupts everything and acts like a tool. Kristen Stewart has an "oops incest" arc that doesn't belong in a kids' movie. Baby Josh Hutcherson makes Botox face, totally unresponsive to the dangers around him — I'm actually going to blame Jon Favreau's directing more than the actor himself.

I like the practical effects, such as the Zorgons.
