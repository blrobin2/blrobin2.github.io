I love the effects, the absurd set pieces, and the devil-may-care pacing that skips several places of connective tissue. The film appears to be a satire of Reagan's deregulation and the 80s fast-food culture. By playing it for laughs, the film evades saying anything substantial. Also, I get bored with sci-fi plots where the military takes over the finale. Not Larry Cohen's best, despite being his best-known
