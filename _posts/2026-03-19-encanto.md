What a beautiful and captivating movie! Watching with someone who A) has Colombian heritage, B) loves Lin-Manuel Miranda, and C) somewhat resembles and sees themselves in Mirabel — these allowed me to soften on some of the flaws and focus on what helps this film stand out from the countless "I don't know what makes me special" kids' movies.

Stephanie Beatriz uses her expressive voice to its fullest. Brooklyn 99 fans may not have seen her in any other context and only know her gruff "Rosa Diaz" persona, but she puts so much heart into Mirabel.

The color is so well considered. The film manages to be vibrant and multicolored while still having a defined palette. It's very clear how much art direction went into this one, and it shines for it.

It's hard to talk about without risking spoilers. I will elaborate in the SPOILER SECTION below. Still, this one made me unexpectedly tear up in a couple of spots and was better than what I expected for a 2020s Disney movie.

Making Charise Castro Smith a co-director so that she doesn't get full credit is disappointing, to say the least.

#### SPOILER SECTION

I love that the film has no true villain. Of course, the film portrays Bruno as the scapegoat, but it's pretty clear early on that he was innocent. Abuela is possibly the closest, both in terms of who causes the most trouble and who "reads" the most Disney villain.

I love that the film expresses that our gifts are not what make us special or worth loving. Mirabel's journey is not about discovering some magic she already had, but about embracing her deep, abiding love and letting it heal the family.

That said, I don't appreciate how much of the onus for healing the family is put on Mirabel's shoulders. The film shifts that onus slightly as it progresses, but it's hard not to walk away with the toxic, all-too-familiar message: "I must love my family no matter what."

I LOVE that the resolution was the community coming together to rebuild, and the suggestion that it is the community that holds the magic together.
