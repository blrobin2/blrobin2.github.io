The ice in Greenland looks painted. The raven looked almost CGI in the way the filmmakers lit it. The filmmaking is stunning, injecting so much color and expressiveness into a film full of so much lament. The Kalaallisut language has such a poetic cadence.

The short feels like it contains a brief glimpse into Inuit culture. But what comes to the fore is how it captures a more global (but by no means universal) experience — that of an older sibling doing their best to protect the younger from the pain they've experienced. The silence in place of lies to hide what is in plain sight.
