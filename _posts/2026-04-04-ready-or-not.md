Seeing this in theaters when it first came out was a romping good time — the audience was game for the misanthropic murder of innocent people caught in the web of rich people's insanity.

Watching it again, the seams show a bit more, such as the clunky exposition and the dreadful amount of runtime dedicated to explaining the rules, the history, the relationships, and so on. Also, the plot contrivances that we go through because the writers ran out of ideas become apparent as we step through certain scene types repeatedly (trying my best to avoid spoilers).

Still, Samara Weaving is as iconic a final girl as one could ask for. Her scream is primal, and she never escapes without taking a lick (or worse). It has a decent pace, and the violence (mostly) makes an impact.

I see no world where this needed a sequel, but I will suspend judgment, just in case.
