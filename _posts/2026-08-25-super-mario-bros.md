I still remember my dad's silence as we left the theater, and feeling for the first time that movies could be bad.

And yet, I found the movie oddly compelling—perhaps because it fit so poorly with the Mario I knew, I did so much mental gymnastics trying to make the universes cohere. To be fair, the movie does make quite a few references to the games, but not always in intuitive ways. I thought about the ending throughout my childhood, waiting for that sequel that couldn't possibly come.

So many tremendous actors — Bob Hoskins, John Leguizamo, Dennis Hopper, Fiona Shaw — are giving some of their career-worst performances. But they still, against all odds, make this movie work in a way that it otherwise wouldn't.

It's awful. But also so weird and kind of gross. My heart can only feel love for it.

The Rifftrax guys are fixated on continuity errors this episode, but they're all pretty easy to explain. Like the boots have cartridges, so Mario and Luigi can't use them willy-nilly.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- The animated opening looks like a 90s PC "game" where you learned random facts about dinosaurs.
- The score does a tremendous job of convincing the audience they're watching a funny, structured movie.
- The case that holds the dinosaur egg with baby Daisy inside felt so artistic and elegant.
- This movie taught me as a child that there are different types of water, including tap water.
- The alternate dimension is slimy, dinosaur-laden cyberpunk. I want so much more of this world!
- I LOVE how red Mario's bedroom is.
- This movie makes it canon that their names are Mario Mario and Luigi Mario.
- The devo chamber process is horrifying.
- I just realized that Dennis Hopper holds his arms like a T-Rex for large parts of this movie.
- Why does Koopa bathe in mud like a Harkonnen?
- This movie has the first version of "Love is the Drug" I heard, so the original still sounds weird to me.
- I always felt bad when Mario left Bertha on the dance floor. Yeah, she stole the rock, but you guys were vibing!
- "Walk the Dinosaur" is a bop.
- I don't care, I love their red and green outfits
- I wonder if this movie inspired the ice slide level in Super Mario 64?
- I love the needless continuity of Koopa ordering a pizza, then someone telling him his pizza has arrived over the radio while he corners Mario Bros. and Co.
- Lance Henriksen cameo as The King
