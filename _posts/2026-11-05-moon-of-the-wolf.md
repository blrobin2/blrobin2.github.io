The movie has a slow, procedural quality that I find kind of cozy, even if it doesn't really go anywhere. Some folks will likely find this hella boring. David Janssen seems to be going for Gumbo Columbo. There is nary a mention of a werewolf for the first hour of this 75-minute movie. The cop accomplishes absolutely nothing.

British riffers. They self-edit their swearing with a harmonica. It may be the most obnoxious thing ever done on a RiffTrax.
