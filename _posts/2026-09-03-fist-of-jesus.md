Haven't watched this since it came out — I remember not loving it, and watching it now, I feel the same. It largely has to do with how indulgent it is — the kills are amusing at first, but it just keeps going, never really topping itself after the first 2/3 of it. Still, if you can't get enough zombie content and haven't watched this, it's better than some of the lazy, cheap ones.
