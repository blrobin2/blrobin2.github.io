<b><a href="https://boxd.it/qa26g/detail" rel="nofollow">Cult Movie Challenge 2019 | 8/52 | Bleeding Skull</a></b>

<b>From the visionary director of <i>Troll 2</i> and <a href="https://boxd.it/8GyQoz" rel="nofollow"><i>Night Killer</i></a> comes a movie about a man and a dog, kind of.</b>

We open on Vince Raven's music video for "Identity Crisis," where he dons several costumes. The camera pulls out and meets up with Vince, his girl Sandra, and the film crew watching the video on a portable cassette player. Vince hated the "Identity Crisis" video, so they're heading to his childhood house for some "hometown inspiration."

At home, the caretaker, Jos, prepares to welcome Vince home. He stops when he hears scratching noises outside. He steps out to find a pack of stray dogs snarling at him. On the road, Vince and the gang hit a roadblock and a warning of dogs and five deaths in the area. The sheriff recognizes Vince and reminds him that folks don't remember his father too fondly.

The sheriff, tempting fate, pulls a gun and calls for the dogs to come out. His deputy soon finds him mauled and dead. Then, the monster dog takes the deputy, too. Vince accidentally hits a German Shepherd. Vince puts the dog out of its misery by bashing its skull in. A bloodied older man emerges from the woods, warning them that they have summoned the wrath of the dogs.

The full moon peaks from behind the clouds. The monster dog will command the hounds. Will anyone survive their rampage? What did Vince's dad do?

The film has an artistic direction. It's cheap, it's gory, and, at times, entertaining! Alice Cooper, in the starring role, gets the movie an extra half-star.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- This Bleeding Skull remaster looks so nice! It's a shame they blu-rays are out of print.
- Alice Cooper wanted to be in a cheap, direct-to-video movie, and this script fit the bill. He ended up being the most expensive part of the budget. It's so wild that he did two songs for this movie that were only released on his B-sides album. I mean, they're silly as hell, so I understand why he didn't put them on any major releases.
- I'm embarrassed to report that Alice Cooper, in a cowboy outfit, works for me.
- "You were more full of water than the Hoover Dam. And look at you now, a rock singer!"
- I love the way the crazed man says, "You will all die!" before skittering off into the woods.
- Angela has a nightmare, wearing a shirt that says, "Dream."
- — "Werewolves do exist." — "Oh, bullshit, Vince. The year 2000 is right around the corner. I am a recognized expert in electronic videos. And you are the hottest rock 'n' roll star in the world. You're making records, videos, movies on high-tech electronic equipment of fantastic sophistication. You could be on a plane tonight and be in Australia tomorrow. And you're scared of werewolves?"
- The way Alice Cooper gently pets Victoria Vera's hair so as not to mess it up.
- "The Raven" is a bop! Oh, it's an Alan Parsons Project song?
- "See Me in the Mirror" is the other song Alice Cooper wrote for this movie. It's got some giallo shimmer!
- Dude drops the f-slur and immediately gets a fire poker to the foot. Hell yeah.
- I am pleased to report that Alice Cooper turning into a werewolf does nothing for me.
