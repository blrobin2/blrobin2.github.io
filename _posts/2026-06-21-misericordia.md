Jéréme is back in town for a funeral. Why is he there? What is his relationship to the people around him? Who wants to fight him and who wants to fuck him? That's all I can give you before the movie spins off into its wild web of unknowable characters and bizarre circumstances.

If you've seen <i>Stranger by the Lake</i>, you'll understand in part what I mean, but this movie operates on an other, more comedic wavelength. Not barrels of laughs, but unexpected bursts at the humor of the situation.

The film is very much not for everyone. While I found it compelling throughout, the person I watched it with found it boring and aimless. I just like watching a movie where I'm not sure what's going to happen next — that doesn't happen all too often anymore.
