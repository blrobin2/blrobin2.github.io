Man, to go back and pick 2018 me's brain. I was so on the fence about this movie! The third act escalation felt so out of left field at the time.

But it’s clear on this watch just how logical a leap it is. It's still batshit, but coherent batshit. And of course I'm a sucker for the Gondry pastiche.

I do think the humor is dated — not in an "oh no."
sense, but more in a "of its time" sense of humor. Like, <i><a href="https://boxd.it/KBIU" rel="nofollow">I Love Boosters</a> </i>isn't as funny but has bigger laughs.

I think what may have been controversial to folks (beyond the third act escalation) is that there are no good people in the movie. Everyone has a flaw that would be a dealbreaker for some people.

As much as we want to cheer for Detroit, her art operates in the same tone-deaf, tonal abstraction as Cassius' (initial) actions do — they both obscure the issue by centering themselves. As important as Squeeze is to the cause, he's still messing around with someone in a relationship.

But no one in the fight is going to be perfect — and if we believe that, then no one will step up because we'll think we're too \_\_\_ to help.
