Mystery author Cornelia Van Gorder, along with her lesbian partner, Lizzie, discover that a legendary murderer has returned to the home they're renting. Cornelia is on the case!

The film is an honest-to-god proto-slasher, with a breezy pace, plenty of action, and some fun performances. Sure, you'll figure out whodunnit before the movie does, but when it goes down so smoothly, it's just nice to see a mystery with a coherent internal logic.
