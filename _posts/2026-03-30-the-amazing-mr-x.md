This movie's first act has impeccable vibes! Very Gothic, a little noire, and an air of mystery. As the movie progresses, it falls a bit into the "men explain to women how irrational they're being" nonsense. It also makes a couple of choices near the end that seem there only for last-minute shake-ups, rather than for any meaningful plot reason.

That said, I enjoyed the performances throughout, and the cinematography was gorgeous! And I'm saying that after watching a crappy direct transfer on DVD — I can't imagine how it would look with a little polish and care.

Not for everyone, but if you enjoy 40s movies, you'll probably get something from the experience.
