On my second viewing of Romy and Michelle, I have little to add to my original review, other than that the dance sequence to "Time After Time" is truly iconic.

Also, the consumerism/skinny culture really came through. Oh, to be as thin as Romy, drinking Coca-Cola and eating junk food, and still "dieting" before the reunion. Your dreams can come true, too — all you need is to have a boy from high school who had a massive crush on you come into a ton of money and finance you with no strings attached!

Still, this movie is cozy as hell. Yes, I bought it on blu-ray.
