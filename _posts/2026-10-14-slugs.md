If only this movie lived up to its first act! I love the tonally disparate score, the outrageous gore, and the unnecessary amount of explosions. But as often happens, the plot gets in the way and slows the thrill ride. Also, there's a SA scene in the third act that does nothing for the movie—I could read it as a reflection of the town's negligence affecting the wrong people, but that might be too generous.
