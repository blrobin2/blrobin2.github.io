Dr. Cardan is passed out in front of the TV. He dreams of pages torn from an ancient text fluttering in the wind. A pair of boots stalking a woman down a hallway. Claws. The police arrive on the scene as the woman tries to call the elevator. The cops run up the stairs, shooting the door handle off to enter. But they're too late — the monster rips the woman apart in the elevator. They shoot at the monster, but nothing seems to stop him. Dr. Cardan wakes up.

The following morning, Halloween, Dr. Cardan receives word that Devlon had escaped and was killed—Cardan believes that Devlon, despite killing 17 people, is not criminally insane, but a Satan worshiper and a demon. Cardan insists the body be cremated. Meanwhile, med student Jorge tells Oscar the lie he told Olivia and friends to get them to come to an old house for a Halloween party, claiming that the place is a luxurious mansion. At the house, the women realize it is far from luxurious and that the whole thing was a ruse.

To appease the understandably enraged women, Jorge searches the house for something interesting. In the attic, he finds a glowing book of Satanic incantations, the name Devlon imprinted on it. The women are, once again, understandably enraged that Jorge is messing with it, but the boys want to up the ante and use Satan's power to resurrect someone from the dead. They'll steal a body from the morgue and take it to the cemetery.

Meanwhile, some middle-school-aged kids out trick-or-treating hitch a ride to the cemetery. You see where this is going, right?

Sure, it's derivative. But it's got vibes. I don't know how to explain it.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- Men always suck in horror movies, but these guys are especially heinous. Like, few slashers ever make the teens so directly responsible for their demise.
- This movie is so padded—I couldn't accuse a single shot of being short. Shots will follow long walks, slow approaches, boats riding away, and so on. This movie could be 10 minutes shorter just from those accumulated seconds.
- The score has the beautifully cliché piano roll sting. I never hate them.
- Of all the bodies in the morgue, why do they take Devlon? Because he is the "ugliest one."
- Opening a shaken-up champagne bottle is a solid way to get an authentic response on camera.
- I can't decide whether they should have waited to kill Jorge, because he's the one we want to see dead the most.
- Dude is just ripping people's guts out.
- Ooo, AND the house is possessed? Okay, movie, I'm in.
- Dude, the third act rules! These zombies look sick.
- While it's cool the movie puts kids in danger, they could've really raised the ante and killed one of them.
- Damn, this movie is too long.
