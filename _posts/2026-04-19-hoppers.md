For a film featuring talking animals and advanced sci-fi technology, the most fantastical element is its politics. While the threat of violence was one of the strongest forces in changing the power dynamics at play, the arc of the film is ultimately a system slowly tearing down a powerful woman and calling it growth.

I deeply appreciated how central nature and community were to the story. I cried when the movie was about being a part of something bigger and connecting to the larger world. Also, the film was unafraid of raising the stakes, taking the story to intense (and, frankly, ridiculous) places. The bones of this are not too dissimilar from a "nature run amok" flick, but that would require consequences that Disney/Pixar would not approve of.
