<blockquote><i>I don't think they want to be the art—they want to be the artists.</i></blockquote>

I'm struggling to find the right words! The film felt powerful and hilarious. I love the stop-motion and creature effects. The colors are so vibrant and intoxicating, and the performances are delightful and game for the absurdity at play.

The film's resolution, while earnest, didn't feel like it matched the energy it was building—I suspect the movie made some compromises to secure the distribution it did. Still, the core message is the same: we feel alone by design, and can find meaning in our collective struggle.

<blockquote><i>We're all one side of the same contradiction.</i></blockquote>
