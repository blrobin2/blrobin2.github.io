A perfect cast in a docudrama satire baked in a 90s family movie shell. Due to Waters' trash-core beginnings, his transition to higher-budget films hasn't always succeeded in retaining his bite. This film, however, captures his sardonic wit, giving us a cast of characters full of hate and malice who differ from Beverly only in the strength of their convictions. She may be guilty, but no one is innocent (even Sam Waterson's doofy Eugene takes out some anger on an unsuspecting dental patient). The only difference between Beverly's murders and the state's capital punishment is the latter's unilateral support from Christians.
