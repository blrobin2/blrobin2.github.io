Dolemite plays cards in a prison cell. He's serving twenty years for some stolen furs and half a mil worth of narcotics. Through a flashback, we learn that Dolemite's rival, Willie Green, set Dolemite up. A guard calls Dolemite into the warden's office, where Mama Queen Bee awaits. She's been working for two years to prove Dolemite's innocence.

Queen Bee gives word that the drug problem in Dolemite's neighborhood has only worsened since he went to prison. Worse, some gang killed his nephew, Little Jimmy. So, what's the plan? The governor has approved putting Dolemite undercover to figure out who is behind the drugs and violence.

The film follows Dolemite, with the help of his kung-fu fighting sex workers known as the Dolemite Girls, as they take revenge on the corruption that put Dolemite in prison.

The film is chasing vibes, where Dolemite is essentially Rudy Ray Moore's rendition of James Bond: a karate-kicking everyman whom every woman wants to fuck, everyone good loves, and everyone evil wishes to denounce. I am far from the best person to explain the history of how Dolemite came to be. I recommend <i><a href="https://boxd.it/je6E">Dolemite Is My Name</a></i> — it's not an amazing film, but it's an entertaining history.

<blockquote><i>I'm gonna let them know that Dolemite is my name, and fucking up motherfuckers is my game.</i></blockquote>

Rudy Ray Moore has such a sincere energy that it's hard to hate, even though the movie itself is a mixed bag.
