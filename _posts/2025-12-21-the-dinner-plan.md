The short has a great cast. I miss seeing Patrick's dad in his videos. I have a hard time with a premise where it's all about the protagonist manipulating everyone, and the stakes are entirely in their head. Although I got some chuckles at moments, the genuinely disgusting (bordering on fetishistic) finale washed away any goodwill I had.
