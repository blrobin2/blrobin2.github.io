Another movie I didn't get the first time I saw — I followed Ebert's line of thinking that it was just another classic TV show recast for the 90s.

But this script does a wonderful job of blending the sensibilities and plotlines of the original show with drag-culture-esque humor and queer characters who get happy endings. And the double entendres are non-stop!

Of course, the star of the movie is Jennifer Elise Cox, whose pitch-perfect "Eve Plumb as Jan" performance and internal demons create so many iconic moments. Don't get me wrong, Christine Taylor's Marsha is hilarious, and everyone else does a tremendous job.

Thank you, <a href="https://boxd.it/fbFszz" rel="nofollow">John Early</a>, for encouraging me to rewatch and reevaluate this movie. I totally see the parallels between Maddie/Marsha and Deena/Noreen!
