Mary Jo and Bridget riff on this <a href="https://boxd.it/5aKhKn" rel="nofollow">MST3K revisit</a>.

I thought I might have been too harsh on this movie the first time around, because the first act is solid — but the endless cycling between Jan's power monologues and Dan cruising for bodies really dragged this one down. This has about an episode of television's worth of material stretched to its limit.
