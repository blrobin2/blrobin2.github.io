Yet another movie where a woman has unexplainable experiences, and people accuse her of being hysterical.

But what I appreciated was how the hysteria spread to the townspeople. Even the doctor, the man of science, calls his beliefs into question. Only the preacher is steadfast, what with his superstition regarding god and whatnot.

That said, it's not all that exciting, and their characters are tropes at best, except for Ruth Ford's character, who actually had a POV.
