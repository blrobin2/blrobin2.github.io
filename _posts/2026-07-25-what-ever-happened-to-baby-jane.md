Mary Jo & Bridget riff

Not to be confused with <a href="https://boxd.it/3Pa7x5" title="the original 1962 version">the original 1962 version</a>, this version, featuring the sisters Vanessa and Lynn Redgrave, is so much darker and sadder. I felt bad laughing at the riffs!

Mary Jo & Bridget do their own silly rendition of Blanche and Jane Hudson. It's fun to see them on screen again.
