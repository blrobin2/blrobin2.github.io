A friend of mine has been reading Parker Posey's autobiography but hasn't seen a single one of her movies. I recommended this one to my friend and decided I'm ready to rewatch it myself.

I love all these neurotic weirdos and all the specificity that the actors bring to their characters.

- Meg and Hamilton were so lucky to be raised around catalogs.
- Will Sasso's obsession with fishing is such a wholesome character choice.
- Poor Gerry.
- Jennifer Coolidge doesn't have to say anything to make me laugh.
- Max Berman is a nightmare human. I'm horrified by the idea of finding myself in a room alone with Larry Miller, but knowing my luck...
- I don't know why, but Scott and Stefan hanging up all that stuff in their hotel room really stressed me out!
- Fred Willard playing close to self as a pervert with no filter.
