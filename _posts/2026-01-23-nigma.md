At Saint Mary's College in Boston, an all-girls boarding school, Tom and Kim help Kathy get ready for a date. They put ill-matched colors on her face and slipped an oversized dress on her. Her date is with Fred Vernon, the gym teacher, who promised to take her dancing but ended up stopping the car in the woods instead. When she confesses her feelings for Fred, the kids hear everything and turn on their headlights, laughing.

Kathy realizes it's a setup to make fun of her, and she runs. The kids chase after her in their cars, sending Kathy running into the road, where an oncoming vehicle hits her. Kathy goes to the hospital, where she is in a coma. The nurse watches as all the lines go flat. She calls in Dr. Anderson, but nothing seems to work. We hear Kathy's voice, somewhere, begging not to die. Her spirit appears to float upwards, floating over the city.

New student Eva arrives at Saint Mary's. Several kids watch from their windows as the headmistress, Miss Jones, greets her. As Eva walks up the stairs, she shifts between herself and Kathy. Eva had a nervous breakdown and has to start school later in the year. The headmistress gives Eva Kathy's old room, along with Kathy's old roommate, Jenny. Eva's one goal this semester is to make out with as many cute boys as possible.

During aerobics class, Eva makes sweet eyes at Mr. Vernon, drawing his attention. She feigns a pulled hamstring and asks Mr. Vernon for a massage. They set up a date, against Jenny's recommendation. The cleaner, Mary, hangs around despite Mr. Vernon asking her to leave. As she turns a corner, her eyes glow red. We soon learn that Mary is Kathy's mother. When Fred looks in the mirror, his reflection bursts out of the mirror and strangles him. Kathy's vitals at the hospital go crazy.

<blockquote><i>How does a girl who is brain-dead experience a violent emotion?</i></blockquote>

Fulci has said as much that this film is an homage to <i><a href="https://boxd.it/1pYN3r">Carrie</a></i>. It also has aspects of Argento's work, a la <i><a href="https://boxd.it/4V7Xsd">Suspiria</a></i> or <i>Phenomena</i>, with a girl's school and a girl with unusual abilities. Here, it seems that Kathy has possessed Eva and is using her to do her bidding. But is there a person beneath, or is Eva a manifestation of Kathy's desire to live?

As many have said, this isn't peak Fulci, but it's still pretty good! The plot is kind of whatever, but the kills are nuts, and the practical effects are right in that sweet spot where they don't gross me out but they're convincing enough. Oh, and the score has some perfectly cheesy synth guitars.
