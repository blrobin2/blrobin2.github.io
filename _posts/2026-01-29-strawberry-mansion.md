I appreciate the atmosphere this movie creates. I love the idea of spending time in other people's dreams and sharing them. I hate the idea of taxing dreams, <b>especially when they have ads in them already!</b>

I don't think the movie gave me enough grounding in the characters to fall for them, but I appreciate the imaginative journey they take. Perhaps it ends up feeling a little sugary by the end. Not a bad thing — quite a tasty treat — but the unmoored fluffiness kept me from loving it.
