<blockquote><i>I must tell you that we really have no desire to conquer any cosmos. We want to extend Earth up to its borders. We don't know what to do with other worlds. We don't need other worlds. We need a mirror.</i></blockquote>

<blockquote><i>When man is happy, the meaning of life and other eternal themes rarely interest him.</i></blockquote>

I remember so many scenes from this movie, but when I saw it in my 20s, I didn't understand what the movie was about. I could feel the movie exploring what it means to be human, even if I didn't have the life experience to inform the nuances.

Like many Tarkovsky films, I saw the sadness and found it profound in its own right. But I saw this time more: that meaning is made in the doing. That art speaks loudest to us when it reflects some aspect of our experience. And, most importantly, that if we were to find out what would make us happy, we may only see our own reflection looking back at us.
