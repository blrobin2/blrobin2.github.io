A man shaves while a bound woman, Alana, screams out behind him. Next to her are two suitcases: one with assorted weapons, and another with Santa Claus masks. Santa enters a room with a man bound in Christmas lights and picks up an axe. The bound man, Kevin, assumes Santa is Alana's cuckolded husband. Instead of the axe, Santa flips a switch, and all the Christmas lights electrocute Kevin, his eyes exploding.

Deputy Aubrey Bradimore wakes to a call from Sheriff Cooper. Deputy Kevin Jordan is MIA, so she must work Christmas Eve. She apologizes to her parents and heads out. Her first job is handling a mall Santa, making kids feel bad and telling them that Santa isn't real (his name is Epstein). When she finds out Alana is missing too, she goes to Alana's to find her in pieces around the room.

Who is this mysterious Santa and why is he so full of hate?

I didn't realize that, going in, this was a <i>Silent Night, Deadly Night</i> reboot. Besides being about a killer Santa, I don't see how it ties in, but I've only seen the first two in the series. The film is chasing a particular edgelord horror fan who hates people. The majority of people in this film are total dicks. The kills are brutal, and the targets are anywhere from sex workers to a 14-year-old girl (the latter is shocked to death with a cattle prod).

The movie has a sense of humor, but its jokes are about Santa being a pedophile, SA, or something racist. Aubrey's "hero's journey" seems to be proving that she's not too traumatized after her husband's death to solve a murder case.

Crazy gore, competent filmmaking, awful humor — YMMV.
