Despite coming out in the 70s, this movie feels like the filmmakers used 50s technology. It starts so slowly, padding so much of its runtime with the protagonist flirting menacingly. Once it gets a plot going and establishes some conventions, the movie gets more bearable.

The effects are so cheap — I love the miniature explosions. The zombies look like someone is applying a clay face mask too aggressively.

The hero is first and foremost a misogynist womanizer, second a writer, and finally, inexplicably, an expert in aircraft, voodoo, and shooting reptiles.
