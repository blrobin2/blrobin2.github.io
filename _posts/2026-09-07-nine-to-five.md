Dolly Parton joins in the comedic stylings of Jane Fonda and Lily Tomlin, and she knocks it out of the park. If you're looking for realism, look elsewhere — let me dream about a world where workplace equality and employee happiness are as important as the bottom line.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- So many wonderful outfits!
- Mr. Hart is such a creep straight out of the gate.
- Dictaphone
- — I'm a married woman. — And I'm a married man. That's what makes it so perfect!
- "Several people have lost their hearing in this room!"
- Doralee's gun speech hits so hard.
- "Atta girl"
- I LOVE the dream/fantasy sequences, especially the Disney/cartoon one.
- I don't like Violet's baby blue Buick Skylark. It just doesn't feel like the right color for the car.
- I always forget how episodic this movie is.
- The movie really hits its peak when they start making changes at the office. Sadly, this movie's progressive notions of workplace equality have only become more of a pipe dream in the US.
- "If I want to have an affair, play sex games or M&M's, you can't stop me!"
- Violet's "where are they now?" makes perfect sense. Everyone else's sort of breaks the movie's reality.
