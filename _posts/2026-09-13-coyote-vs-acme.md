Low-effort Alamo pre-show material, including a repeated ad for the Road Runner show.

I got a lot of laughs out of <a href="https://boxd.it/97B2gf" rel="nofollow"><i>The Day the Earth Blew Up</i></a> — here, it was more respect for the bits than outright hilarity. Thankfully, the number of kids laughing in the audience helped me appreciate that, after all these years, something fundamental about Wiley E. Coyote's deal still appeals to kids.

I loved seeing all the Looney Tunes characters (Tweetie would absolutely be a narc), though it felt so bizarre that people didn't know who Bugs Bunny was. I also enjoyed how insanely destructive the ACME products were — I'm such a child when it comes to cars blowing up and getting crushed by boulders.

Like <i>Barbie</i>, the movie is Warner Bros. saying "We recognize that corporations do evil things!" without actually having to interrogate that idea too deeply. I appreciate the theme of "losing isn't the same thing as failing." The movie makes one choice that undermines its whole premise, but I will take arguments to the contrary (iykyk).
