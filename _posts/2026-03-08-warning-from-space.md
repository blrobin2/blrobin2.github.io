Riffs by the awful British duo. I tried to find some jokes I appreciated, but nothing really tickled me.

#### Favorite Riffs

- That remark is funnier in my language.
- What is this? Some kind of warning from space?
- Let's go to sleep — big day of not existing tomorrow!
