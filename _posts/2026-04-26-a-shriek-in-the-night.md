Wow, I never thought I'd see Lyle Talbot outside of an Ed Wood movie! The film is a rather straightforward whodunit, but Ginger Rogers brings some spunk that keeps the movie afloat.

The mean-spirited humor definitely dampened the fun in places — two character's whole deal is that they are "dumb." But one of them gets a redemption in their own way.

Louise Beaver gets about as good a role as one would expect for a black woman in the 30s, but that doesn't make it any less disappointing.

The lighting and sets are pretty excellent, especially when the shadows lean noir and exaggerated.

Your mileage may vary! But I mostly enjoyed this.
