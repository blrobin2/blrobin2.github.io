Baron von Frankenstein works with his assistant, Otto, to develop something that embodies the ideals of the Serbian race. Most important is that what they make has a correctly Serbian nose. They will look among the townspeople for this ideal. While heading to the lab, they encounter Baroness Katrin Frankenstein, who complains about the school where she enrolled her children. The Baron cuts her off, telling her to remind him at dinner.

The stableboy Nicholas and his friend Sacha dig holes, not knowing what they're for. Sacha is considering becoming a monk, so Nicholas decides he'll take Sacha to a brothel to dissuade him. While out for a picnic with the kids, Katrin catches Nicholas having sex with a woman. Enraged, she demands that he go to the castle and speak to her later.

In the lab, Otto brings a dead woman out of a tank of water on a gurney and lowers her onto an operating table. They build a male body from their favorite disparate parts, but still need a head. The Baron believes it should be the head of a man who strongly craves women, so that his head will fall in love with the woman they have put together. They will mate and create the ideal new race that responds only to the Baron's bidding.

The Baron sends Otto to the local brothel to find a suitable head. What is a Frankenstein movie if Frankenstein's assistant gets the wrong head?

<blockquote><i>Soon, here in Serbia, in my laboratory, perfection will become a reality!</i></blockquote>

The Baron and Baroness are both obsessed with genetic superiority. The Baron is obsessed with the Serbians, especially their noses, because he believes they are descendants of the Greeks. The Baroness believes her children are superior to every other child, special and beautiful.

Both are also sexually repressed. The Baron is disgusted by the "ill-proportioned" bodies of women at the brothel. He compares them to his sister and wife, the Baroness, whom he ignores during his fixation. The Baroness, then, finds in Nicholas a stud who can satisfy her wants. In both cases, it is a selfish obsession that actively despises the other party involved.

Thankfully, we have Nicholas, who, despite being a horny moron, still recognizes how fucked up all of this is. We are never meant to have any sympathies for the Frankensteins.

The film implies that Sacha is gay — hence, he is not interested in having sex with women, alive or dead. While at the brothel, Sacha watches Nicholas have sex more than he pays attention to the sex worker.

The gore in the movie is quite graphic and well done. Some shots were quite nauseating and disturbing — some of it is so absurd I can't help but laugh, maybe from the sheer discomfort of it all.

<blockquote><i>To know death, you have to fuck life… in the gallbladder!</i></blockquote>

As gross as this movie is in all its facets, it's also an honest depiction of bourgeois obsession with domination and subjugation. Whether to enslave for labor or sex, it's all about the fetishization of otherness. When given the choice between liberation and assimilation, the petite bourgeoisie always chooses the latter, trading identity for proximity to power. They think it buys them safety, but that safety is illusory.

It's wild just how much more focused, composed, and entertaining this movie is compared to <a href="https://boxd.it/a8Z6CT" rel="nofollow"><i>Blood for Dracula</i></a>. It's still rough around the edges, but it has a clear arc to its story that <i>BfD</i> was missing.

- Nicoletta Elmi, who plays Monica Frankenstein, was in so many horror movies!
- Their dining room table is cartoonishly long. Poor Olga, the maid, has to walk the food back and forth along the table to serve them.
