Every time I watch this, I want to like it more than I do. I enjoy deconstructing all the pieces of <i>Star Wars</i> and other sci-fi that they incorporate, assuming that everything is as close to the original as possible while avoiding copyright infringement.

I still laugh at the reveal of the first ship, the chestbuster's Michigan J. Frog routine, and Mr. Radar sitting next to Mr. Coffee. The meta comedy gets an occasional snort. As a kid, I thought, "Your schwartz is as big as mine" was peak comedy.

I hate the characterization of the princess, especially when she is based on Princess Leia — it's just boring misogyny that also plagues other parts of the movie.
