That Godzilla Theme hits us so hard as a typhoon blasts Kurata Coast, knocking down power poles and deteriorating land and structures. The following morning, Reporter Ichiro and his photographer, Junko, arrive to capture photos that match the tone of Ichiro's report. Amimoto, the village leader, arrives to complain about Ichiro's report, which contradicts Amimoto's promise to finish development in the region by the target date.

Reports come in of a giant egg floating off the coast of Nishi Beach. A kannushi arrives to offer prayers to the workers to protect them from curses, thus allowing them to fish near the foreboding egg and bring it ashore. Kyonan University sends Dr. Miura to investigate the egg. Mr. Kumayama of Happy Enterprises arrives on the scene, having bought the egg from the villagers to display it for an admission fee.

Back at the hotel, Ichiro, Junko, and Miura plot a way to save the egg from Kumayama's clutches. Kumayama meets with financial backer Jiro, who plans to display the egg in his new amusement park, Shizunoura Happy Center. The Shobijin of Infant Island appeal to Ichiro, Junko, and Miura to help recover the egg, as it belongs to Mothra. On the island, it is their greatest hope — here in Japan, it will bring destruction.

The film follows the egg, Godzilla's return, and the people's appeal to Mothra to save them once again.

Thematically, the film explores private property through Mothra's egg—what naturally occurring phenomena can capitalists lay claim to? What resources can be provided as public services? People in this movie are more scared of losing their jobs than they are of Godzilla's imminent attack. Don't worry, this is Godzilla—the capitalists get their comeuppance.

Also, this movie makes literal what was once metaphor: Godzilla is divine retribution for the nuclear testing humanity has performed. Infant Island is in such a desolate state because of those nuclear tests. Mothra is the messenger of peace, the reminder and protector, even when humanity doesn't deserve it.

Mothra is the best kaiju because she's cute and kind.
