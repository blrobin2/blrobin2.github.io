This movie is baffling! I don't understand its score choices, its random cinematography choices, or even the story. Like, someone sees someone fall on the dance floor, and it gives them PTSD?? The lighting is awful — I could barely see anyone's outfits, even the ones covered in sequins!

This movie is only so bad because it starts so well, then gets much worse. Was this a miniseries cut down into a movie? I don't understand.
