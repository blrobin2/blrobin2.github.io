Daniel Clamp, a multimedia business tycoon, tries to buy Mr. Wing's business to build a highway through Chinatown. After Mr. Wing dies, Gizmo must run, but he gets caught.

Billy and Kate work in the Clamp Building. Billy does architectural designs, while Kate runs tours. At Slice of Life Inc., a lab that rents out one of its floors, Dr. Catheter studies anything and everything. A scientist who cloned himself finds Gizmo and brings him in for study.

The delivery man hums the song Gizmo sings, and Billy overhears. Billy rescues Gizmo, opening the monkey cage doors. He tries to get Gizmo to hide. Yadda yadda yadda, he leaves Gizmo to his own devices. Gizmo gets wet. Chaos ensues.

So much sillier and more creative than the first. It really leans into the cartoon aspects — silly sound effects, more expressive Gremlins, and so many wacky visual gags. Rick Baker and team really knocked this one out of the park.

It makes sense that Amblin was involved—the humor is so meta, referencing other WB IP and the previous Gremlins movie. We also get opening and closing Looney Tunes segments directed by Chuck Jones!

The one part that has aged pretty poorly is Hulk Hogan's cameo, but it made perfect sense in 1990.

Am I overrating this? Kind of! But, like, who cares?
