<b><a href="https://boxd.it/UBBe4" rel="nofollow">Film Theory and Criticism | Rudolf Arnheim</a></b>

The Erik Satie score rules — I love the passages where it seems to get "stuck."

The film manipulations ranged from a little hack to awe-inspiring. The multiple exposure juxtapositions were aesthetically pleasing but ultimately overwhelming. The slow-motion chase after the hearse and the boat on the sea of roofs were mesmerizing! It’s fun to see Clair and crew try to distort the film in as many ways as they can think of.

I like how we see the end of the movie before it actually ends.

I applied a sort of death-and-resurrection of Jesus on top of these images to try to build some sense out of it, but I may be reducing the focus with such a narrow interpretation.
