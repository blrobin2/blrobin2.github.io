Mike riffs with Joel McHale. They keep using the "queers" in a pejorative sense. Yes, that word was rampant in the '80s. But please don't use it like that, even in jest.

I'm not opposed to a WWIII skirmish fought by teenagers — after all, the sudden Russian invasion in this film is not too different from US invasions, like, everywhere else. If seen through the lens of critiquing American foreign policy, I can get behind it.

The thing is, though, MGM and John Milius were explicitly collaborating with conservative think tanks to make a jingoistic teen <i>Rambo</i>. How else could they triple their budget and get the pro-war, pro-torture action set pieces that make up this movie? The result is a long trek through unearned gravitas. It's just not that interesting of a movie. But boy, is it violent!

It's weird running into William Smith twice in one week. He's some Russian leader in this, and the lead in <a href="https://boxd.it/fd4gVR"><i>Seven</i></a>.
