<blockquote><i>You got a real kind face. The kind of face I'd like to forget!</i> — Norm MacDonald or Felix the Cat??</blockquote>

What a bizarre oddity! So much effort in the animation, but altogether so janky. The 80s loved sword & sorcery so much they threw it into <a href="https://boxd.it/eMsO8f" rel="nofollow"><i>Felix the Cat: the Movie</i></a>. Each character feels like it had a different animator working on it. Every hand that touched this movie was laced with LSD. I know so little about Felix that I don't know what, if anything, was fan service. Like, is Felix actually known for his magic bag? Don't tell me.
