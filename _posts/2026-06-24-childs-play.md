Cops are useless. Andy's a tough dude. Karen kicks ass as a single mom who can't practice her art because she has to work a shitty retail job to support her and her son—she never stopped fighting for her kid without falling into cliche. Maybe I'm keeping my score a little high to spite the 14-year-old I watched this with who said it was 2 stars.
