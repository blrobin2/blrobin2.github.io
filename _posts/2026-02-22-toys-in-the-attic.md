I'm not sure what kids would get out of this, but visually, it's a remarkable achievement that kept me engaged throughout. So much nightmare fuel! Story-wise, I'm a little lost.

The film seems to have more on its mind than at first appearances. The toys all have non-Czech accents, so they are the immigrants. The statue is of a former dictator, surrounded by antiquated, distorted, or, in the case of the blackface toy, racist toys. The dictator seems to want to destroy the immigrants, but to possess and clone the "beautiful woman" doll.

That said, it could also be that the white-coded, aesthetically pretty characters are the good guys, and the disfigured and non-white toys were the bad guys.

What does it all add up to? I don't know ¯\_(ツ)\_/¯ Perhaps I'm not Czech enough to understand the nuances.
