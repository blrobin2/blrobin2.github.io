<blockquote><i>On April 1st, 1973, the most bizarre and macabre event in all of Brooklyn's modern history occurred. Twelve people, inhabitants of a reputed house of prostitution and an illegal abortion clinic, were killed. Only one occupant survived. When found by police, she told a tale so fantastic and horrific, she was believed to be insane. Authorities immediately placed her in an insane asylum. The most brilliant investigators spent years trying to solve this gruesome mystery… but to this day, are still baffled. Could the rantings of a girl, supposedly insane, be true? The makers of this film believe so.</i></blockquote>

Rebecca has what the doctors call Hyper REM Neurosis. She frequently dreams about men taking her to a BDSM hospital or killing her in her sleep. She was the sole survivor of the illegal abortion clinic massacre.

It started with Rebecca's boyfriend, Bill, dragging her to an illegal abortion clinic. Rebecca believes that abortion is wrong and that she'll give up the baby for adoption, but Bill isn't hearing it. They arrive and ask for Big Mama. While they wait, Candy, another customer in the waiting room, asks Rebecca if this is her first abortion, Candy stating that she's already had three.

Big Mama invites Rebecca back to her office and offers her a drink, insisting. Rebecca believes that they're just going to talk so she can appease Bill. Big Mama, looking like the villain principal in a kids' movie, assures Rebecca that it's better to wait until the time is right.

<blockquote><i>— I won't kill it.<br>
— This thing you think you're killing has no eyes, no fingers. It ain't human. Not yet. This is the easy way out. Real easy.</i></blockquote>

Rebecca feels lightheaded. Big Mama drugged her. She never had a choice. Big Mama performs what she calls the "biggest second-trimester abortion yet" and gives the fetus to Bertha to flush down the toilet. We follow it down into the sewers, where it comes into contact with toxic waste leaking into the sewer. The fetus screams like a baby and mutates.

<blockquote><i>She'll be alright. Big Mama knows what she's doing.</i></blockquote>

I, uh, think the movie is anti-abortion. And it's pretty awful. But holy shit, these special effects are so rubbery and goopy! And it has its own twisted sense of humor.

If I were making a horror parody, it wouldn't be that different from this. It's genuinely impossible to know how sincere these filmmakers are.
