This movie is deeply stupid, but mostly tedious. It has a budget and a sense of humor, but both are poorly placed and tough to recognize. Rifftrax added a laugh track to one scene, and it really helped.

Nearly every single shot is a Dutch angle — it is so hard to tell what is happening in each shot.

I feel like the alien design was discovered on a Korn fan's MySpace page as their interpretation of Goku.

This one actually hurt physically.
