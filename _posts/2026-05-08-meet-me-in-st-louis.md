Wow, this movie charmed me! I knew it was gonna be a classic, but I didn't expect to have such heartfelt moments that land. The Halloween segment is one of my favorite parts of any musical. So much joy and melancholy. So many strong-willed, nuanced women!

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- This movie is to 1903 what contemporary movies are to the 80s — it's a cartoonish rendition, but still familiar
- Ugh, imagine if trains were good!
- Judy Garland's eyebrows are so cunty!
- The colors look sooooo nice in this! Not too saturated and crisp
- Tootie is such a goth — she loves talking about death — "I buried Maude Rockefeller today. You missed all the fun!"
- The dad sucks! He ruins everything.
- The more I see Judy Garland, the more I understand early ball culture
- Ugh, the cakewalk song is hella racist
- I gotta start calling things "ginger peachy."
- In 1903, turning the lights down was enough to get a boy hot and bothered
- The Victorian house is so gothic! I don't know how they make it feel more Halloween-y!
- "She died in a den of thieves, and I died of a broken heart." Tootie is my favorite, she's so maudlin!
- In 1903, Halloween was the purge for kids — they made a bonfire in the middle of the street!
- They're the scariest girls in the neighborhood!
- Who is the boy dressed in drag with the mustache supposed to be??
- I love the tracking shot of Tootie, horrified to flour Mr. Braukoff!
- "I'm the most horrible!"
- John Truitt LOVES getting beat up — "If you're not busy tomorrow, you mind beating me up again?"
- "You got a mighty strong grip for a boy!"
- "We'll be safe with me — I got 12 guns in my room!"
- "I absolutely hate, loathe, and despise money." Same!
- The dad sucks!!
- That fox fur stole is ugh.
- "I don't hate you, I just hate basketball!" Same!
- I didn't realize "Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas" was from this movie!
- Yes, girl, destroy those asbestos snowpeople
- The dad FINALLY realizing that making decisions without his family is, uh, bad!
