<blockquote><i>Infinity lives in the moment and sails on a whim, without permission or insurance of any kind, and all on a shoestring budget.</i></blockquote>

A group of wealthy white dudes, self-proclaimed "sea gypsies," sail from New Zealand to Patagonia with a stop in Antarctica on the way. The twist? They're a bunch of amateurs. Well, except for the captain, Clem, who seems to be the one leading all these rich morons on an adventure.

We do get brief moments of cool footage — underwater reefs, volcanic eruptions, schools of dolphins, ice shelves, and so on. They also try to do some respectable charity work, such as cleaning up plastic on beaches, but they quickly realize how little their efforts matter when so much plastic is washing up on the beaches daily. They acknowledge that fishing companies are depleting this area of fish.

But we also get them yelling at seals and shoving them away so they can land their dingy. One person on the boat catches a coconut crab and talks about how delicious the crabs are. Hard cut to him saying, "Uh, they're endangered, so we're not gonna eat him." They also meet up with another ship with a woman on board, and the things that come out of these men's mouths…

Add to all this the fact that most of their dangers are self-inflicted, and it's hard to root for these men.
