Watching this with someone who also has Ehlers-Danlos syndrome, they confirmed the short is a solid introduction, but misses out on deeper levels of the experience, such as prolapsed organs and regular surgeries. But in terms of the lightning-storm of pain and the stonewalled doctors who won't do anything with the given symptoms, this short captures that quite beautifully.
