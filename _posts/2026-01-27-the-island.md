The first half is a pretty good combo of <i>Logan's Run</i> with a dash of <i>Never Let Me Go</i>, filtered through a Gen X Soft Club aesthetic. The second half is a Michael Bay action movie. We get all the ableism, gay panic, and misogyny you've come to expect from Bay. The color saturation and shaky cam seemed designed to induce nausea, but maybe I'm just accustomed to the bland tones of contemporary action movies.

I initially checked it out because Jasmin mentioned the similarities to part of <i><a href="https://boxd.it/czkMoh">Cloud Atlas</a></i>. I had a list of quips I made throughout the movie, but I lost it. So, guess you had to be there. Sorry!
