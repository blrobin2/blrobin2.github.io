Melvin Junko AKA The Toxic Avenger, has become the hero New Jersey needs, cleaning up Tromaville of all evil and wrongdoing. Today, he lives in a garden apartment in the Tromaville dump. His blind girlfriend, Claire, gets him a job as concierge and athletic director at the Tromaville Center for the Blind.

But Tromaville's peace and serenity are short-lived. Apocalypse, Inc. sends in a spy dressed as a PUS driver with a package secretly containing a ticking time bomb. They intend to replace the spot with a toxic chemical storage area. When the PUS driver shoots up a blind older woman in a wheelchair, Claire runs outside, surviving the bombing that killed everyone else.

From the rubble emerges The Toxic Avenger, mop first, to kill the assailants. It's a ten-minute sequence of some of the most absurd and idiotic kills I've seen. So the remaining Apocalypse, Inc. execs retreat to lick their wounds and plot their takeover of Tromaville and the destruction of The Toxic Avenger. It involves sending Melvin to Tokyo to look for his birth father.

It's Troma, so the gore is over-the-top, and the humor is juvenile edgy nonsense. The film is less engaging than its predecessor, partly because it gets so excited to be in Japan that it forgets to do much. The movie is funniest when it does Looney Tunes-esque stupidity.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- The Toxic Avenger theme is sort of a bop.
- Two dudes getting their heads shoved together and blowing up.
- The guys shooting machine guns at the Toxic Avenger, but accidentally point them at each other. It's played like a Looney Tunes cartoon where they both realize they've shot each other to death and curse while collapsing.
- The steam blowing out of his ears during the sex scene.
- The most offensive thing is Michael Hertz, Lloyd Kaufman, and Patricia Kaufman dubbing the Japanese actors who couldn't speak English clearly. Of course they have to put on awful accents.
- Apocalypse, Inc. gets people in Tromaville hooked on smoking crap.
- — Neither a borrower nor a lender be. Shakespeare! — Fuck you! David Mamet.
- The fishmonger accidentally chopping up Big Mac is actually some of the most brutal body horror.
- Sumo wrestling to Amazing Grace is so weird.
- The third act has a ridiculous amount of explosions. I'm not mad.
