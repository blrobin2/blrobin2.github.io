Phew! This movie had some shocking moments that caught me off guard. The cantaloupe scene will haunt me for a while. I do think this movie has a sadistic edge that I don't love, but I can't complain too much when the visceral impact is this strong.

What gives this movie weight is the genuine emotions on display from the excellent performances. I wasn't prepared to cry for this, but I was fully invested in Piper's journey. Sora Wong plays a challenging role as someone vulnerable in so many ways, lied to so many times that she cannot tell whether her brother is lying without a codeword, and yet still displays agency and intuition. Sally Hawkins reminded me a little too much of my mom at times, so kudos to her for such an intense performance.

Yes, you can poke at the logic of Laura's process or the choices characters made in duress. And yes, we see the smattering of influences in the story's patchwork. Still, I have a high regard for movies that touch on child abuse with this degree of authenticity, especially when the horror elements are so heightened.
