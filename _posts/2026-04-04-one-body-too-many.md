What a fun little movie! I'm always down for a dark house mystery. Inject some classic humor, and I'm sold. The pacing is just right — I never felt myself drifting, even when the plot went through the familiar beats.

Jack Haley (of <a href="https://boxd.it/bvmWLZ" title="Tin Man">Tin Man</a> fame) gives a delightfully goofy performance — perhaps too broad in places, but plays a bit like a character on <i>Frasier</i>. Bela Lugosi has a minor role, but he makes each appearance count. The rest of the cast are folks you've almost certainly seen in another film, and they're all solid and game.

Sure, I've seen it before! But when it works on me, it works.
