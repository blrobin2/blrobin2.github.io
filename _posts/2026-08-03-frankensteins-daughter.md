The men are so egregious! This film has maybe the most misogynist men (heroes and villains) that I've seen in a movie from this era.

There's a music break with teens in bathing suits — did they need to make a beach party movie connection with John Ashley in it? Also, I thought Sandra Knight played <a href="https://letterboxd.com/films/in/the-gidget-collection/by/release-earliest/size/large/" rel="nofollow">Gidget</a> in one of those movies, but I was mistaken.
