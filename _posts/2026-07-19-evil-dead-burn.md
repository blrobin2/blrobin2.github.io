I don't know how I feel about this. On one hand, this movie captures the relentlessness of the original Evil Dead series. But I kind of don't like that pacing — it's exhausting and numbs me out.

Also, I appreciate the levity with which the film depicted domestic abuse—the mother is the textbook enabler who looks the other way in the name of keeping the family together.

That said, I could feel the difference in violence between what the men experienced onscreen versus the women. I'm sure it’s imbalanced like this in a ton of things I've watched, but the gore is so visceral and grotesque that the film seems to delight in their disfigurement.

In that way, the film feels like an homage to the New French Extreme from the 00s, with its fixations on gross violence in domestic spaces. I don't know if this film has the thematic depth that (some of) those films did.

A good horror movie, to me, is one that I could watch again despite the intensity of the experience. This movie makes me question if I even like horror movies anymore.
