Judging by the amount of penises, I reckon I watched the director's cut.

This movie has been one of my blind spots for nearly 20 years. And for a comedy that old, it's aged (mostly) well. More importantly, the tired music-biopic tropes and clichés this movie called out are still alive, with only Weird Al willing to challenge the status quo. Yes, it's dumb as hell, but, lord help me, so am I.
