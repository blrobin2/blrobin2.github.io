I've now seen 3/5 of child actor Bryon Taylor's filmography. This is one of two movies he starred in which features fantasy characters find their way into the present to fight their rival's hijinks (the other is, of course, <a href="https://boxd.it/dCc9nD" rel="nofollow"><i>The Sorcerer's Apprentice</i></a>). He also collaborated with director Paul Matthews on three other films: <i>The Fairy King</i>, <i>Berserker: Hell's Warrior</i>, and <a href="https://boxd.it/cx3ofX" rel="nofollow"><i>The Little Unicorn</i></a>. You know, just in case this comes up in trivia.

Merlin is played by the same guy who played the titular <i>Drop Dead Fred</i>. He looks more than a little like Simon Pegg.

I love cheap 2000s CGI!
