It's April Fools Day at Doddsville County High School. Popular Carol leads nerdy Marty to the women's locker room under the pretense of having sex. She has him undress in a shower stall while the camera crew sets up. Marty pulls back the curtain to kids spraying him with a fire extinguisher, chanting April Fools. The kids keep tormenting him in elaborate ways until the coach catches on and punishes them all.

Ted and Carl skip the coach's punishment and offer Marty a joint to say "No hard feelings." Marty heads to the chem lab to work on a science project. He lights up the joint with a Bunsen burner, choking on whatever chemical the boys added to make him sick. Still, Marty persists in his chem project, pouring a big ol flask full of nitric acid. When the nausea gets too much, Skip, the crew's leader, adds some powder to Marty's experiment.

When Marty returns, a chain reaction goes off and sets the whole table on fire. He tries to put it out, but nitric acid falls from the cabinet all over Marty's face, and the whole lab explodes. The crew runs to the locker to see the disfigured and badly burnt Marty screaming in agony. As the stretcher carries Marty away, Carol tries to apologize, but Marty reaches up to strangle her. Or did he?

Carol wakes up from the nightmare. Ten years have passed, and the high school reunion is approaching. The crew runs into each other outside the school, which looks vacant and run-down. Undeterred, they break in. Who set this up? I mean, obviously, it was Marty.

This film occupies a special place in low-quality horror in that the entire premise is Jigsaw-level manipulation of space and time. Everyone is intrinsically evil and hates one another, making it incredibly easy to root for the killer. Like, even as people are dying, they're blaming their friends. Its hangout vibe is not great for slashers, but it made this one more enjoyable.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- The electric guitar-tinged score is so jarring compared to the otherwise synth score. I guess the filmmakers thought Harry Manfredini was going to give them that Friday the 13th magic, but it appears that wasn't in the budget.
- It takes 40 minutes for the first kill. And of course it's the Black caretaker.
- These kids are full of complex pranks to play on one another!
- The gore is cheap but goes for broke, with thick blood and weird-looking internal organs falling out of bodies.
- Sometimes, you just take a bath at the abandoned high school you broke into.
- Oh Jesus, total skin melting off! They just find her skeleton.
- A reunion "you're the father" declaration — classic.
- Since when does April Fool's end at noon?
- And why would Marty so doggedly hold to that rule?
- Whoa, when the nightmare of the ghosts haunting Marty comes on, this movie really shot up for me.
- Wait, the whole thing is Marty's dream during the skin grafting surgery??
- Slow-mo ripping off his skin graft.
