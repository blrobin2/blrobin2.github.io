What does the poet love most of all? It is Death — that which motivates all poetry. For life has no meaning without the presence of Death to give it a finite shape — a negative theology built on what cannot be true. It is when Death interferes with reality and the life of the poet Orpheus that meaning distorts and love subsists on half-measures.

The film takes the visual motifs that Cocteau established in <a href="https://boxd.it/fzIU8t" rel="nofollow"><i>The Blood of a Poet,</i></a> exploring similar themes surrounding death and obsession while applying them to the Orpheus myth. The result is a stunning visual rendering that maintains Cocteau's dream-like worlds within a reality not too dissimilar from our own.

It’s wonderful to see so many images that have inspired later filmmakers, and most exciting that the images still retain all their captivating power.
