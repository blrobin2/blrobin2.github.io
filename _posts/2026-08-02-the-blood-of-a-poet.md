The artist comes into being through the pain of existence, sublimating the pain instead of succumbing to it. They turn inwards, to their past, distorted through eternal recurrence, forced to relive the heartache and regret. The images come at times in flashes, and at others in nightmares. The subconscious becomes the nightwatchman, reminding them so that it never happens again. But it happens every time they look in the mirror, every time they fall asleep. What has left them behind in reality lives on in the destructive immortality of memory.
