<blockquote><i> Let's go get sushi and not pay!</i></blockquote>

I first saw this movie at a late-night movie event at a local coffee shop. I also saw <i>Re-Animator</i> there. I didn't remember much from the movie except for the perfect ending. What I forgot was how funny, remarkably quotable, and character-rich the whole experience is.

<blockquote><i>Don't tell me to fuck off! You ain't earned the right!</i></blockquote>

Owen assured me that Alex Cox's films tend to have a nihilistic bent — this one has no redeemable characters, except maybe Miller. But the film feels authentically part of the punk community, an aesthetic that so many movies have tried for and failed. Like, the only other director I feel captured a certain scene as effectively is Gregg Araki.

<blockquote><i>I blame society.</i></blockquote>

I think I'll watch this one again pretty soon.
