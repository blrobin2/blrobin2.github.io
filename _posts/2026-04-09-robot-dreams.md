In the first act, I thought this might be a little fluffy, but I found it endearing. As the film progressed, I felt a growing Don Bluth-esque emotional weight.

The film isn't afraid to show that the world can both be supremely kind and unreasonably cruel. And one of the cruelest aspects of life is the passage of time and our total lack of control over it.

We don't ever earn friendship — we give it reciprocally for as long and as much as we are able.
