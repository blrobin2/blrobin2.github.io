A tonal chef's kiss, brilliantly balancing comedy and sincerity through a consistent, elegant melodrama lens. Every sensitive subject it touches, it does so deeply and empathetically.

I may have clocked the John Waters influence, but I would not have pinpointed <a href="https://boxd.it/fbZZ87" rel="nofollow"><i>The Brady Bunch Movie</i></a> had it not been for John Early's Alamo Drafthouse intro.

I was tearing up in a couple of moments, perhaps because my cauliflower bites showed up 3/4 of the way through the movie and I finally felt sated. But this movie also hits an emotional core that resonates with me.
