<b>Hong Kong 1986</b>

Lily waits outside as her mother has sex with Uncle Kai. Lily's father, Kwan, notices Lily outside. He takes her upstairs, along with his henchman, Nam. Lily's mother claims that Kai forced it on her, so Kwan and Nam beat him up. Kwan grabs some clippers to castrate Kai, but Kai turns the tide, killing Kwan, Nam, and Kwan's wife. Kai notices Lily hiding. He douses her with kerosene and lights a match, but someone walks in and interrupts, sparing Lily's life.

<b>Johannesburg 1996</b>

Kai goes on the run, and works in a Chinese restaurant. Kai is disgusting and disgruntled, putting spit and other bodily fluids into the food. Meanwhile, Lily has grown up to become a flight attendant. After landing in Johannesburg, she and some friends go to Kai's restaurant. As Kai walks through, Lily feels sick and goes to throw up. She flashes back to her parents' murder and has nightmares of it. Still, her shitty boyfriend convinces her that it was only a dream.

Kai's boss, Kei's, wife bullies Kai, but Kei keeps Kai around because he'll do anything for money. Kai and Kei are tired of the butcher's prices, so they drive out to see the Zulu tribe to buy some cheap meat. There, they discover several tribespeople sick with Ebola. While driving home with pigs, Kai accidentally crashes the car. While Kei fixes it, Kai wanders off, finding a Zulu woman dying from Ebola. Kai SAs her and kills her.

All that to set up that Kai is a living Ebola carrier who spreads the disease through his gross sources and interactions with the food. Will anyone be able to stop Kai before he wreaks total havoc?

<blockquote><i>God created Ebola, not me. And his virus will kill all you bastards!</i></blockquote>

Anthony Wong is so ridiculous in this. It's much clearer than <i><a href="https://boxd.it/b0lhDf">The Untold Story</a></i> that this movie is going for comedy, despite being about some fucked up stuff. In addition to the simulated violence and diseased skin, we also watch extended scenes of Kai chopping up live frogs and chickens being sacrificed. I could really do without all the SA and animal violence.

The movie becomes more entertaining when it's less about Kai's depravity and more about the Ebola breakout. The graphics of the disease living in his mouth are so silly, as well as all the slo-mo scenes as we see Kai infect or nearly infect people. His final moments are the movie's peak.

<blockquote><i>You bastards, how dare you bully me? Ebola!</i></blockquote>

An argument could be made for the film being about class politics and wage slavery, but someone smarter than me would need to make that argument.

I think you'll know from how I described the movie whether it's up your alley.
