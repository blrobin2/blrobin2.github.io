<blockquote><i>It's not my job to do the emotional labor of making the audience laugh</i></blockquote>

Benny Feldman is my favorite comedian, maybe ever, after this special. Having seen him live, I thought I had seen all this material, but he front-loads the show with his greatest hits. The middle of the show is a tonally perfect takedown of the current right-wing comedy scene, using its own irony against it while threading sincere observations throughout.

For those unfamiliar with Benny Feldman, his jokes are mostly one-liners, and many rely heavily on irony or knowledge of online discourse. Also, several gross sex and poop jokes are going to be off-putting for some folks — I thought about showing this to a couple of folks until certain sections came up that I thought they might recoil from.

For me, though, this is the most hilarious and cathartic comedy special I've seen in ages.
