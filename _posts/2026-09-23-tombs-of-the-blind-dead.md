The Knights Templar carry an incapacitated woman through the castle streets. They strap the woman to a cross, ripping her clothes off for a sacrifice. Men on horseback cut at her, then gather around to drink her blood.

Cut to present-day Spain, where Virginia runs into her old friend Betty. Betty ruins a mannequin factory out by the old church cemetery. Virginia's friend Roger meets them and invites Betty camping, telling her to meet them at the train station.

On the train, Roger hits on Betty, inciting Virginia's jealousy. A flashback suggests Betty and Virginia dated. Distraught, Virginia jumps off the train. Roger pulls the brake alarm, but the conductor refuses to stop in this area. Virginia finds the ruins of Berzano, with a graveyard full of ankhs instead of crosses. She looks around for anyone living who can help her.

Finally, she finds some branches and a pit and decides to light a fire. As night falls, the graves rock and smoke emanates from them. Skeletal hands lift tombs and hooded figures emerge. What will happen to Virginia? Will Roger and Betty reach her before the blind, dead Knights Templar dudes?

The director saw the blind dead less as zombies and more as mummies with vampiric tendencies. It still amounts to dead things coming to life and killing folks, but it does change what happens to those folks afterward. Having them on horseback makes them scarier—the designs rule (so much so that the director reused the costumes for <a href="https://boxd.it/bja67h" title="The Ghost Galleon"><i>The Ghost Galleon</i></a>).

I enjoy it when they do on-location shooting. The old monastery and ruins look sick. That said, the movie gets a lot of its mileage out of just wandering around the space. The slo-mo horse-riding shots are quite beautiful.

The film explains away Betty's queerness with an undisclosed trauma from her past, then immediately puts her in a totally unjustifiable SA scene (which was cut from the version I saw, but the before and after are still there).

It’s slow, silly, and fairly paint-by-numbers, but I enjoyed the settings and costumes, monster or otherwise.

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- An alternate title for this movie is "Revenge from Planet Ape."
- 70s jump scares are so funny. A random woman screaming. A frightened cat.
- The actress who played Virginia actually did some scary stunts falling off a horse!
- The random organ music stabs are abrupt and short.
- I'm glad we get to see Betty's spooky mannequin factory.
- Betty has no consistent clothing or hairstyle. In one scene she wears a beret and glasses, and it took me a moment to realize it's her.
- I've heard of a canary in a coal mine, but a canary in a morgue?
- Love the creepy Templar paintings, especially the crows eating their eyes.
- Nina setting the eye in the mannequin!
- Betty wears go-go boots to the crypt.
- It seems women in this movie can't open locks.
- And the movie ends with the blind dead gaining access to locomotives—they'll be unstoppable.
