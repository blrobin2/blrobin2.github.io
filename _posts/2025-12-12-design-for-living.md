Pre-Code horny love triangle comedy — while the plot is a little rough-going at times, the movie zips by effortlessly, landing where we wanted it to be all along.

Did you know Gary Cooper is hot? He looks like Christopher Abbott's sexy grandfather. Or great-grandfather, I don't know!

Miriam Hopkins gets much more to do here than she did in <a href="https://boxd.it/bfT3L3" title="Trouble in Paradise">Trouble in Paradise</a>, but that movie is nearly flawless.

This movie was someone's bisexual awakening in the 1930s.
