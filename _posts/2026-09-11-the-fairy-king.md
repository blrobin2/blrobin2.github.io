Really putting a dent in Paul Matthews' filmography.

The first 5 minutes of this movie show a woman reading from a storybook to two kids, then smash-cut to a van driving for a while.

The dad is so wildly hell-bent on draining the fairy swamp. To what end? Gold.

The CGI for the fairies is perfect. True nightmare fuel.
