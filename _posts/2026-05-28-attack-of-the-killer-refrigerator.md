<b><a href="https://boxd.it/qa26g">Cult Movie Challenge 2019 | 17/52 | Shot on Video</a></b>

Teens throw a kegger. The next day, Lori grabs a beer from the refrigerator, noticing how loud it is. She complains to one of the roommates, and he tells her about the party, where they messed around with the fridge by knocking the ice off the freezer box. Now, he believes the fridge hates them. He might be right.

The shot-on-video documentary approach is actually pretty brilliant for grounding the movie. It's only when the movie tries for more cinematic… cinematography that it pulls me out. From what I can tell, they filmed a party and told them the premise, then let folks sort of mess around. Then they filmed later footage with someone telling the story of what happened at that party to try to pull the footage together.

Still, it has its charms, obviously going for silly over serious. The gore effects look solid on video! I can't say I understood HOW folks were dying half the time, but I don't mind. Either they bought some music or Jeffrey Majeau had some underutilized skills.
