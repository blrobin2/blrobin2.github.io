One of the rare, but not unheard of, Rifftrax redos of MST3K movies. It's been long enough that I don't remember much of this, but it started coming back as I watched it. I stand by my original assessment — kind of dig it, even if it's nothing remarkable.

#### Favorite Riffs

- Dragon trainer? If only there had been a series of movies to help train him in his craft!
- If you know where she is, why are you bothering the pool of magic?!
- I was a volunteer from my church to guide you through these dangerous lands, but good luck and God bless you on your journeys.
- Mount your horses, gentlemen, and come see for yourselves, unless you don't care to dine at the finest Cracker Barrel in all the realm.
- Many a brave knight was lost that day for lack of a pool noodle.
- Yep, nothing says "Hero's moment of triumph" like Transylvanian organ music.
- Forward, gentlemen, and keep in mind that I am not evil in any way.
- Get me my ring, and I'll dispose of these men in quick order, as soon as I decode Little Orphan Annie's secret message.
- Magic. Of course, the boy has magic — explains how my mixed nuts mysteriously turned into a springy snake!
- Thank god for horses' natural asbestos coating!
