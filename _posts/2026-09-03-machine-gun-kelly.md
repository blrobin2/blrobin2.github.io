George Kelly walks down Main St dressed in workman attire, a large case in hand. A car pulls up to pick him up and stops at Lebanon Bank. George and Howard enter the bank. George gets the money while George shoots up the guards with his Tommy gun. While they drive off, George stuffs the cash into a case and disassembles his gun. They drop the money off at a drop point.

The driver drops George off, and he hobbles over to Flo's convertible, laughing at the ruse they just pulled. Flo gets pulled over. The cop asks to search the car — George gives him a hard time:

<blockquote><i>Go ahead, search the car. Search us, too. You think those brass buttons you got give you a hunting permit on respectable citizens, don't you? You search 'em, push 'em, eye up their women.</i></blockquote>

But the officers get a call telling them to narrow their search elsewhere. When George and Flo get back to the hideout, the money is $11K short. George Kelly just became a public enemy. How long will his attitude keep him alive?

<blockquote><i>Machine Gun Kelly's my little baby.</i></blockquote>

George's secondary thing, besides loving that machine gun, is being afraid of Death. His first pickup is at a funeral parlor, and he shivers when he realizes it. One of his partners in crime has a statue of Death, and George goes cold just thinking about it. It ends up causing him a lot of trouble, but seeing him freak out over a skull-and-crossbones tattoo is so funny.

This film was Charles Bronson's first lead role, and probably his best performance I've seen. Not his most convincing, but he's most rounded. In his later career, he was so gruff and monotone. Regardless, he's always been good at being thoroughly unlikeable.

By sticking close to conventions, the movie turns a quickie concept into a solid picture, with, like, themes and stuff.  By the third act, we get some solid tension, but the movie doesn't quite know what to do with it. Still, more good than bad!

#### Stray Thoughts / Spoilers

- Cool graphic opening!
- Gerald Fried's jazz score is pretty solid!
- The gas station with the mountain lion bums me out. I've seen one in real life before :( They keep going back to it so George can taunt the mountain lion!!!!
- I love that Flo's parents know all about Kelly and don't care.
- "I picked you up some nice cold chicken and potato salad!" Uh, gee, thanks.
- He calls people "Wizenheimers." I've never heard that before.
