The film is much sillier than I expected, and I'm not 100% sure how intentional that was in some places. It makes the smart choice to bring the parents into knowing about Other Mommy near the beginning so we don't have to deal with tired child-gaslighting tropes. But the movie then struggles to figure out where to go, driving around for set pieces in other locations.

The movie's greatest strength is its messaging about childhood consent. I grew up with my family expecting that any adult who asked for a hug or a kiss got one. Here, Bela has to deal with people like Uncle Frank who won't take no for an answer. And, of course, Other Mommy.

I feel like this movie wanted to be the anti-<i>Poltergeist</i>, with its aging hipster parents (the Blouse "Into Black" needle drop was a nostalgia hit) and its frank relationship issues. Also, the "bringing in the experts" moment feels like a direct response to <i>Poltergeist</i>.

I can understand why folks might like this one more than me, and probably more so the folks who dislike it. It took most of the movie for me to vibe with it, although I never had a bad time.
