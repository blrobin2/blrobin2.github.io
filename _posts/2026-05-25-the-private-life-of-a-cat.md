Relatively straightforward and, dare I say, documentary-adjacent compared to other Maya Deren shorts. Functionally, it operates like an elevated Animal Planet segment about a mother cat and her kittens. The camera injects tension through juxtaposed shots of cats, suggesting continuity of action. The narration almost feels like the filmmakers justifying their decision to make this short by mythologizing cats. And perhaps that's why cats have been represented on film for as long as film has been around — we are endlessly fascinated by them and find it hard to explain why beyond, "They're cute."

And to clarify, I'm not mad — the cats and kittens in this are adorable, and the filmmakers do a wonderful job capturing personality and cherished moments. It’s just also a little quaint.
