The Rifftrax dudes love riffing classics. This is the first time I've heard one of them tell a joke too early (he even admits it when the setup comes after the punchline).

This movie is so much wilder than I remember! Rifftrax used the print in which they cut out George's vision of him wishing he'd never been born. It’s so jarring!

I love the dance contest when the pool opens. I hate it when George steals Mary's robe.

- Probably one of THE most Capra-esque directors.
- Dad would want me to have a smoke right now.
- Even the cars are horny cartoon wolves.
- Damn it, Mary, you got raptured without me!
- South America, just a three-month train ride away.
- Kiss me like you kissed your mom.
- Buffalo Gals — if you can think of a more romantic record, I'd love to hear it.
- He's really a negging pioneer.
- You were gonna go out and conquer the world, but Mothra put a stop to that.
