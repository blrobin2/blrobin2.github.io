Somehow, I keep returning to Paul Matthews. First, it was <a href="https://boxd.it/cx3ofX" rel="nofollow"><i>The Little Unicorn</i></a>, then <a href="https://boxd.it/eErfib" rel="nofollow"><i>Merlin: The Return</i></a>. Now...

It’s wild how this movie tries to set up the principal as a villain because she didn't want faculty and service workers fucking the students.

Ashley is one disgusting dude — I was hoping the movie was setting him up as the first to die, but no, he's the hero. Todd Jensen, who plays Ashley, puts on such a stereotypical tough-guy voice—it’s hilarious. Who he saves and who he kills seems entirely based on who he wants to fuck.

The camera is so hilariously leering — so many long takes of knees, butts, and the like. The monster slime/semen is, uh, everywhere. The digital effects are so cheap, even though the sets and practical effects are relatively decent. Then again, this did come out in 1997.

This movie was made by creeps for creeps. It’s also Paul Matthews best movie.
