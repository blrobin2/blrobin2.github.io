Jack Kroll is an unrepentant asshole who belittles then-up-and-coming directors Agnès Varda and Susan Sontag, all in the name of playing devil's advocate.

He calls Agnès Varda's film "grotesque," and when she rejects the label, he demands an explanation beyond, "I don't agree with that label." He starts labeling the people in the film "grotesque," and Varda calls him out for being racist.

Susan Sontag almost instantly picks up a cigarette to tolerate the experience. Jack Kroll associates with her some writings on Andy Warhol and underground film, to which she immediately reminds him that she had not, but, of course, has opinions on it.

The whole thing is a test in patience and a reminder that, no matter how brilliant a woman is, some man will find a reason to talk down to her. But it is also a reminder than the people have been waiting for the end of the world for as long as we can remember.
