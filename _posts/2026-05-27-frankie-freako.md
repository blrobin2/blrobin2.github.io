<i>Puppetmaster</i> meets <i>Home Alone</i> meets every irony-poisoned fake-bad movie of the past 20 years

My biggest complaint about the movie is the total lack of grounding. Everything is absurd from the first moments, so putting some absurdity on top doesn't feel like it changes the world, even when that absurdity is weirdo puppets.

Also, by being intentionally "bad," it sort of buffers itself from criticism, so my complaints just sound like I'm no fun. And to be totally fair, I deeply related to Connor's 8:30 being too late to be up.

I love the practical effects — puppets and miniatures will always warm my heart. I loved hearing the voices of Dan, Stuart, and Elliott from <i>The Flop House</i> (of course, I wanted more of them). I truly wish I loved the movie more. I support the spirit of it more than the final product.
